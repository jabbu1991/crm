Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Who we are

Real View Creative Studio ("we", "us") is a creative and marketing agency in Kerala, India. We run this website (realviewcreativestudio.in) and provide the software products Real View CRM, Real View Order Tracker and the Restaurant Management Software (together, "the software") at crm.realviewcreativestudio.in.

Information on this website

  • Contact and job forms: the details you type (name, phone or WhatsApp number, business name, message, and for job applications place, role and portfolio link) are placed in a WhatsApp message that you choose to send. Contact-form enquiries are also saved as a lead in our own CRM so we can reply.
  • Cookies and the Meta Pixel: we use the Meta Pixel to measure our ads (for example page views and button clicks). We never send your name, phone number or message text to Meta.

Information in the software

Businesses that use our software (our clients) enter information about their own customers and staff, such as names, phone numbers, addresses, enquiries, orders, bills, warranty details and loyalty points. That information belongs to the client business. We store and process it only to provide the software to that client.

  • Each client business has its own separate workspace. One client cannot see another client's data.
  • Staff accounts are protected by passwords, which are stored only in hashed form.
  • Customer links (order tracking, digital catalogue, points card, bills) show only the information meant for that customer.
  • Deleted leads stay in a Recycle Bin for 30 days and can be restored by the client's admin.

Where data is stored

The software and its data run on cloud servers provided by our hosting provider. A copy of the database is backed up automatically every day to a Google Drive account owned by Real View Creative Studio. Backup copies are kept for 30 days and then deleted.

Use of Google user data

Our software connects to Google Drive only to save its own daily backup files. It requests the drive.file permission, which lets it see and manage only the files it created itself. It cannot read any other files in the Google Drive account.

Real View CRM's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is not used for advertising, is not sold and is not shared with anyone.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with the service providers we need to run the website and the software (such as our hosting provider and Google Drive for backups), or when the law requires it.

Your choices

If you are a customer of one of our clients, please contact that business to see, correct or delete your information. You can also contact us and we will help. Clients can export or delete their data at any time, or ask us to do it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date below.
