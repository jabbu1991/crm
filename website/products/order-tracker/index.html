Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Production board

Every stage as a column. Drag an order to the next stage, filter by product or maker.

Fair queue

Orders line up by the date of the first advance. The owner can move an urgent order to the top, with a reason.

Your own stages

Queue, In Production, Ready for Dispatch, Dispatch, Delivered, or rename and add stages for each product.

Makers and production sheet

Assign a maker when production starts and print an A4 production sheet that shows only the maker's amount.

Expected dates

Expected finish and dispatch dates are calculated automatically from production days and the queue. Late orders turn red.

GST bill and warranty

Add the GST bill number at dispatch. Warranty runs from the bill date, including parts with their own warranty.

Customer tracking link

Each customer gets a link showing their order stage, expected date and, after delivery, the warranty.

Referral points

Every customer gets a unit code. When a new order comes with that code, the referrer earns points.

How it works

Simple enough for every staff member

  1. 1

    Order comes in

    Add it by hand, or a Converted lead in Real View CRM becomes an order by itself.

  2. 2

    Production

    Assign the maker, print the production sheet, track every stage on the board.

  3. 3

    Dispatch

    Enter the GST bill number and date. Warranty starts from the bill date.

  4. 4

    Delivered

    The customer's link shows the warranty and referral points.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does it work with Real View CRM?

Yes. When both are on, a lead marked Converted becomes an order in the queue by itself, and you switch between them with one tap.

Can I use my own stage names?

Yes. Make stage sets with your own names and order, and give each product its own set and production days.

What does the customer see on the link?

The order stage and expected date, and after delivery the warranty days left. Maker details and amounts are never shown.

Can I add my old deliveries?

Yes. Upload them from an Excel template so warranties and customer history are in one place from day one.

Who can see the amounts?

You decide with staff permissions. Order takers see their own orders without amounts.
