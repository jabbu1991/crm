Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

All leads in one place

Name, phone, place, state, product, source, status and notes for every enquiry, with a duplicate warning and full history.

Follow-up reminders

Overdue, today and upcoming follow-ups on one page. Set the next date in one tap: tomorrow, +3 days or +1 week.

One-tap WhatsApp

Open WhatsApp with a ready message from your own templates: welcome, quotation, follow-up, offer and more.

Digital catalogue

A product page link for each customer. You see when they open it, so you call at the right moment.

Staff logins

Each staff member sees only their own leads. The owner sees everything and assigns leads to staff.

Reports

State-wise, product-wise, source-wise and staff-wise lead reports, plus today's leads at a glance.

Your own lists

Rename lead statuses, sources and priorities to match how your business works.

Import and export

Bring old leads in from Excel/CSV and export anytime. Deleted leads stay in a Recycle Bin for 30 days.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. Real View CRM works in the browser on any phone or computer. Each staff member logs in with their own username.

Is my data private?

Yes. Every business gets its own separate workspace. Other businesses can never see your leads.

Can it send WhatsApp messages?

It opens WhatsApp with the message already written for that customer, and your staff press Send. No WhatsApp API or extra charges are needed.

Can I bring my old leads from Excel?

Yes. Download the import template, paste your data and upload. Your leads, projects and follow-up dates come in together.

Can I see a demo first?

Yes. Tap Book a demo and message us on WhatsApp. We will show it with your own kind of leads.
