Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Fast billing

Dine-in, takeaway and delivery. Add items by tap, S.No, barcode or scanner. Cash, UPI, card, split or pay later.

KOT and GST

Kitchen order tickets, hold bills, item-wise GST, inclusive or exclusive, with your GSTIN and FSSAI on the bill.

Printing

58 mm and 80 mm thermal or A4. Bluetooth printing from a phone, or send the bill on WhatsApp.

QR digital menu

A beautiful menu with photos, offers and Veg/Non-veg marks. Choose a theme or use your logo colours. QR per table.

Online orders

Customers order from the menu for dine-in, takeaway or delivery. Staff get a bell, phone notification and badge.

Loyalty points

Points on every bill, refer & earn, birthday filters and a personal points card for each customer.

Expenses and profit

Record expenses with bill photos and see income, expense, profit, top items and staff-wise sales.

Staff permissions

Cashier, manager or custom roles. Discounts, cancellations and old bills only for the people you allow.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which devices does it work on?

Any Android phone, iPhone, tablet or computer with a browser. It can be added to the home screen like an app.

Which printers are supported?

58 mm and 80 mm thermal printers and A4. Phones can print to Bluetooth thermal printers using the RawBT app.

Does it handle GST?

Yes. Turn GST on, add your GSTIN and set the GST % per item, inclusive or exclusive of price.

What if the internet is slow?

The billing screen is built for weak networks: it is light, retries saving by itself and never makes a bill twice.

Can I see a demo first?

Yes. Tap Book a demo and message us on WhatsApp. We will show billing, the QR menu and online orders.
