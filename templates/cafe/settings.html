Bill & printer

📱 Phone + Bluetooth printer

One-time setup on the phone: install "RawBT" from the Play Store, choose Bluetooth and your printer, protocol "ESC/POS general", and the paper width. The first time, Android may ask which app to open: choose RawBT and "Always". RawBT's free version adds a line at the bottom of each receipt; buying its licence removes it.

🖨 How to set up the printer
  • Computer + USB/WiFi printer: install the printer's driver. In the print window choose that printer, Margins: None, "Headers and footers" off. Chrome remembers it after the first time.
  • Tablet/phone + Bluetooth printer: install "RawBT" (free) from the Play Store and pair the printer. When you press Print on a bill, choose RawBT in the printer list.
  • Without a printer, Save (PDF) and WhatsApp always work.

GST
