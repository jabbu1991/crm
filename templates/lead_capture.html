📝 Google Forms Connect

Answers to your Google Form come in as leads (campaign Google Forms). The form needs questions with “Name” and “Phone” in them; other answers go into the lead's notes.

  1. Open your Google Form → ⋮ (top right) → Apps Script.
  2. Delete what is there, paste the script below, tap 💾 Save.
  3. Click ⏰ Triggers → Add trigger → function onSubmit, event From form · On form submit → Save → allow access.
  4. Fill the form once yourself to test: the lead appears in Leads.
